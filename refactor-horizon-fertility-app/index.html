    Why this interpretation?

    Age / not changeable

    Age informs group guidance, not pregnancy odds. No age is inferred if skipped.

    Habits / changeable

    Habits support wellbeing but cannot reverse reproductive ageing.

    Cycle pattern / uncertain

    Variation cannot confirm ovulation or conception, and may not apply to your context.

    Missing context / uncertain

    Important factors are not measured. Limited context is never a low-risk result.

    Evidence and limits

    Not calibrated or clinically validated. A steady line can falsely reassure. No diagnosis or pregnancy prediction. Do not delay care based on Horizon.

    Variation cannot measure ovulation, egg supply, sperm, tubes or embryo quality. Hormonal contraception changes patterns. Two days is a demo cutoff, not a clinical threshold.

    ASRM suggests review after 12 months trying under 35, 6 months from 35, sooner over 40 or with known concerns, for regular unprotected intercourse. Other routes differ. Age-context steps are prompts, not biological curves or gender identities.
